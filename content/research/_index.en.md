My research investigates how to design more efficient computing architectures and systems, combining computer architecture, high-performance computing and memory systems. I explore ways to bring computation closer to data, improve the performance and efficiency of parallel systems, and develop tools to understand and evaluate emerging architectures.

## Research areas

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">
<h3>Computer Architecture</h3>
<p>Design, evaluation and optimization of computer architectures, including processors, interconnects and system organization.</p>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<h3>High-Performance Computing</h3>
<p>Parallel programming, data and thread placement, heterogeneous systems, and application efficiency on high-performance platforms.</p>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<h3>Memory Systems</h3>
<p>Research on caches, memory controllers, DRAM, emerging memory technologies and mechanisms that affect performance, capacity and efficiency.</p>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<h3>In-Memory Processing</h3>
<p>Exploration of architectures that bring or integrate computational operations into the memory subsystem, reducing data movement and aiming for improved efficiency.</p>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<h3>Microarchitectural Simulation</h3>
<p>Modeling and evaluation of architectures through simulators, including detailed simulation and approaches balancing accuracy and speed.</p>
</div>

<div class="research-card">
<h3>Efficient Architectures and Systems</h3>
<p>Investigation of solutions to improve efficiency, scalability and resource utilization in contemporary computing systems.</p>
</div>

</div>

## Research approach

My research combines architectural analysis, solution development, experimentation and systematic evaluation. I use different methodologies — from simulation and modeling to implementation and experiments on real platforms — to investigate limitations, explore design alternatives and understand trade-offs among performance, efficiency and complexity.
