## Career

**Adjunct professor and researcher** — Department of Informatics, Federal University of Paraná (UFPR), Curitiba, Brazil (since 2016).

- **CNPq Research Productivity Fellow — Level 2 (PQ-2).**
- Vice-coordinator of the Scientific Modeling and Computing Center ([NMCC](https://cicti.ufpr.br/nucleos/nmcc/coordenacao/), CICTI/UFPR), 2025–2027.
- Deputy coordinator of the [undergraduate Computer Science program](http://bcc.ufpr.br/) at UFPR (2017–2020).
- Postdoctoral internship — Informatics Institute, Federal University of Rio Grande do Sul (UFRGS), Porto Alegre (2014–2015).
- Temporary professor — Informatics Institute, UFRGS, Porto Alegre (2014–2015).
- PhD — Informatics Institute, UFRGS, Porto Alegre (2009–2014).
- Visiting scholar — The University of Texas at Austin, advised by Yale N. Patt (2011).
- MSc — Informatics Institute, UFRGS, Porto Alegre (2007–2009).
- BSc in Computer Science — São Paulo State University (UNESP), Presidente Prudente (2002–2006).

## Research interests

- **Computer architecture:** processors, memory subsystems and interconnects.
- **High-performance computing:** data and thread affinity, parallel systems and cloud computing.
- **Memory systems:** caches, memory controllers, DRAM and NVM.
- **Microarchitecture simulation:** cycle-accurate and fast simulation.
- **In-memory processing:** intelligent memories capable of computation.
