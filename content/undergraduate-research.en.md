## What is undergraduate research?

Undergraduate research is an academic activity in which undergraduate students learn how to conduct and document scientific research through small research projects. The project is supervised by an advisor, who guides the student throughout the activity. Advisors are usually university faculty members with expertise in the research topic.

## Why participate?

- It is an opportunity to learn what scientific research is and how to carry it out, and to help decide whether to pursue an academic or research career. Research experience can also strengthen applications to graduate programs.
- Universities provide a supportive environment, including digital libraries (such as ACM Digital Library and IEEE Xplore), laboratories, and people with research experience, including advisors and graduate students.
- Working in research groups alongside master's and doctoral students provides opportunities to interact with researchers and learn about different stages of an academic career.
- Research teaches students to deal with unfamiliar problems: identify and analyze challenges, review existing knowledge, adapt solutions, and develop creativity. These skills are valuable both in research and in other professional careers.

## Research scholarships

Research funding agencies, such as CAPES and CNPq, offer scholarships for undergraduate research. With an advisor's help, students may submit project proposals to funding agencies. Faculty members may also have funded projects that provide scholarships for undergraduate research.

As with internships, research complements undergraduate education. Choosing an activity solely based on short-term compensation may mean sacrificing learning opportunities. A solid education often provides greater long-term benefits than temporary compensation.

## Recommendations

- Participate in at least one undergraduate research activity during your degree.
- Look for opportunities to work alongside master's and doctoral students.
- Make the most of your advisor's knowledge and experience.
- Do not settle for the minimum: strive to do something outstanding.
