## Suggested syllabus: Digital Circuits

- Course introduction, digital signals, and signal discretization
- Number systems, bases, and base conversion
- Basic binary arithmetic: addition and subtraction; multiplication
- Boolean algebra, truth tables, and algebraic properties
- Logic factoring and canonical forms
- Logic gates and mapping functions to circuits
- Waveforms and introduction to Logisim
- Karnaugh maps and don't-care bits
- Basic combinational blocks: adders and subtractors
- Combinational blocks: decoders and multiplexers
- Combinational blocks: multipliers, shifters, and comparators
- Combinational blocks: mux-based functions, full adders using half adders, etc.
- Sequential circuits: SR, RS, and D latches
- Sequential circuits: SR and D flip-flops
- Sequential circuits: JK flip-flops
- Finite-state machines: Moore and Mealy models
- Finite-state machine design
- Combinational and sequential circuit design
- Combinational and sequential circuit design
