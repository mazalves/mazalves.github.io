## Suggested syllabus

- Introduction and review of logic and simplification
- Review of combinational circuits
- CMOS implementation, transistors, and logic gates
- CMOS implementation of combinational circuits
- Delays, propagation time, and contamination delay
- RAM memory circuits
- Lab: structural modeling in VHDL
- Lab: modeling combinational circuits
- ALU, adders, and subtractors with and without carry propagation
- Lab: modeling fast adders
- Combinational multiplier
- Sequential circuits, latches, and flip-flops with delays
- Registers and counters
- Lab: modeling sequential circuits
- Moore and Mealy finite-state machines
- Lab: modeling simple counters
- Setup and hold time (clock skew and setup)
- Complex circuit examples
- MIPS: instruction set
- MIPS: ALU and memory instructions
- MIPS: control-flow instructions and iteration
- MIPS: function support, activation records, and recursion
- MIPS: instruction formats (R, I, J) and ADD, ORI, LW, SW, BEQ, and J operations
- MIPS: datapath, timing, and delays
- Memory systems and communication protocols
